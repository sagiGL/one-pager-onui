Seed Executive Briefing · 2026
The Mission
Personalization for how humans absorb the digital world.
The Problem
The signal in every session is small: what this user needs now, what the product needs them to see now. Products deliver it buried in everything else and teams pay the cost in activation, churn, and support.
The Solution
OnUI reads user intent instantly and modifies frontend layouts in real time. The product stays the same, the experience adapts.
Why Now
Commoditized LLMs make reading and acting on unstructured user intent in real time incredibly cheap.
